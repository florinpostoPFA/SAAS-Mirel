# Hello, world

This is the first post on the Posto blog.

We are building **AI assistants for European eCommerce stores** — one assistant per store, trained on the store's own catalog.

## Why a blog

A few simple reasons:

- to share what we ship,
- to document how the assistants work in practice,
- and to give merchants something useful to read while they evaluate us.

## What to expect

Short, practical posts:

1. Behind-the-scenes notes from real store integrations.
2. How we evaluate assistant quality without ceremony.
3. Notes on European data, hosting, and language nuances.

Thanks for reading.
